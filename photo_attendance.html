Photo Attendance

Recognize enrolled students using the connected camera.

Before you start

Make sure students have given permission for their photos to be used in this local project and that their photos have been enrolled.

The camera runs on the computer where the Flask app is running. Press Q in the camera window to stop it.

How it works

1. Enroll student photos from the Students page.

2. Train the face model.

3. Start the camera and check the result on screen.

4. Review recorded attendance on the Attendance page.
